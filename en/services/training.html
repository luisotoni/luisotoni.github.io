About the Service

Practical and results-focused training for IT teams. I offer workshops, bootcamps and technical mentoring in Azure, Microsoft 365 and Power Platform. From basic concepts to Microsoft certification preparation.

What's included:

  • Azure workshops (AZ-900, AZ-104, AZ-305)
  • Microsoft 365 training (MS-900, MS-100/101)
  • Power Platform bootcamps (PL-100, PL-400, PL-600)
  • Microsoft certification preparation
  • Personalized technical mentoring
  • Best practices sessions
  • Hands-on labs and practical exercises
  • Documentation and reference materials
