About the Service

Low-code application development and business process automation with Power Platform. Transform ideas into digital solutions quickly with Power Apps, automate tasks with Power Automate, create dashboards with Power BI and implement AI agents with Copilot Studio.

What's included:

  • Power Apps (Canvas and Model-driven)
  • Power Automate workflows
  • Power BI dashboards and reports
  • Dataverse design and implementation
  • Copilot Studio (AI agents)
  • AI Builder for intelligent automation
  • Custom Connectors
  • Governance and CoE setup
